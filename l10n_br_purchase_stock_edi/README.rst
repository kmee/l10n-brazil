Link between the import of vendor fiscal documents, the purchase link
(``l10n_br_purchase_edi``) and stock (``l10n_br_purchase_stock``). It
installs itself when they are present.

- The receipt move is the origin of an imported document line, together
  with the purchase order line.
- The receipt is filled by the document: the quantity the document
  declares is proposed on the receipt for the operator to review.
- The document line and the bill line are compared with the order and
  the receipt: ordered, received and invoiced quantities, order price,
  and the differences, with tolerances by company. It only warns,
  nothing is blocked.

Usage
=====

When the origin of a line of the imported document is decided, the line
also points to the receipt move of the order line if there is a single
candidate: the received and not yet invoiced move first, otherwise the
only one still to come. Returns are never candidates. The receipts show
on the vendor bill.

On the draft vendor bill, **Fill Receipt from the Document** puts on the
receipt the quantity the document declares, for the receipts that nobody
picked yet. The operator reviews and validates the receipt; nothing is
validated by the button.

The lines of the document and of the bill show the ordered and received
quantity, the order price and the differences. A line out of the
tolerances of the company (Bill Price Tolerance and Bill Quantity
Tolerance, in percentage, zero by default, in the company form) is
marked as divergent, the document and the bill get a badge, and the
lists have a filter. A bill issued before the receipt is awaiting the
receipt, which is not a divergence.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Lots and expiry dates declared by the document (``rastro`` of the
  NF-e) are not proposed on the receipt yet: the import does not read
  them.
- Receipts as a source of the reconciliation screen of
  ``l10n_br_purchase_edi``.
- Receipt created from the document for a stockable product bought
  without an order, and the mark of a line without stock entry.
- The divergence is not stored, so the filters of the lists read the
  lines linked to an order line; measure it on a large database before
  storing and indexing it.
