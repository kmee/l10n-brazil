This module reports the medicine group of the NF-e items (group K,
``med``) and enforces the traceability group that the SEFAZ requires for
medicines.

The ANVISA code (``cProdANVISA``), the exemption reason
(``xMotivoIsencao``) and the maximum consumer price (``vPMC``) are set
once on the product and copied to the fiscal document lines of that
product. The values can be changed on each line, and the lines of an
incoming NF-e keep the values of the supplier XML.

The ANVISA code is checked against the layout: 11 or 13 digits, or
``ISENTO`` for medicines exempt from registration.

Rule K01-20 (rejection 873) requires the traceability group (``rastro``)
on every item with the medicine group. The module refuses to confirm
such an NF-e without lots, except in the cases listed by the rule:
complementary, adjustment and return NF-e, non presential sales
(``indPres`` 2 or 3), incoming NF-e, DANFE type 6 and the CFOP of future
delivery and sale on behalf of a third party (5922, 6922, 5118 to 5120,
6118 to 6120). The lots come from the ``l10n_br_nfe_rastro`` module.

Configuration
=============

On the product form, *Fiscal* tab, fill the *Medicine (ANVISA)* field
with the ANVISA code and the maximum consumer price. Track the product
by lot (see ``l10n_br_nfe_rastro``).

Usage
=====

Invoice a medicine as usual. The invoice line shows the medicine group
in the *Medicine* tab and the lots in the *Traceability* tab; the NF-e
XML carries the ``med`` and ``rastro`` tags of the item.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Rule K01-10 (medicine NCM 3001 to 3006 requires the medicine group) is
  not enforced: the SEFAZ suspended it and lists it as future
  implementation.
- The medicine group is shared by the products that use it: changing the
  maximum consumer price changes it for the draft documents too.
