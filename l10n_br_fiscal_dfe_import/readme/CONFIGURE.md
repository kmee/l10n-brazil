The import window and its menu are available to the fiscal users, who import,
review and resolve the lines without the NFe Manager group: the module grants
them the technical record of the payment detail of the note, and records the
supplier de-para and the acknowledgement event with elevated rights only for
that technical step, keeping the author and the company rules. Generating the
invoice still needs the accounting rights. The company of the recipient of each
note must be selected in the company switcher.
