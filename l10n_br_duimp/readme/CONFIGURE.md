1. Upload the e-CPF digital certificate of the person representing the
   company in the *e-CPF Certificate* field of the *Certificates* tab of
   the company form (`l10n_br_fiscal_certificate` module). An e-CNPJ
   certificate is rejected by the Portal Único Siscomex for this profile
   (HTTP 422, code PLAT-ER2008).
2. In the *DUIMP* tab of the company form, set the Siscomex environment
   to use (Company Validation or Production).
